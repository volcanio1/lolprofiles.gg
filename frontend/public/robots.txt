# lolprofiles.gg — crawler policy
#
# Player reports (/profile) and live games (/live) are per-visitor lookups keyed
# by query string: thin, unbounded, and rate-limited against a shared Riot key,
# so they are kept out of the index. `/test` is an internal asset-inspection
# page. Everything else — the landing page, champion builds, the policy pages —
# is fair game.

User-agent: *
Allow: /
Disallow: /test
Disallow: /profile
Disallow: /live

Sitemap: https://lolprofiles.gg/sitemap.xml
